四水归堂 Four Waters Return to the Hall

Tsinghua School of Architecture × miHoYo competition · 2026

A first-person environmental puzzle game set in an old Huizhou courtyard house. You come home after years away and repair the four eaves so the rain can flow back into the courtyard. When the pool fills and turns to a mirror, you step through the reflection into the evening when the whole family still lived there.

~8,700lines of gameplay C++
2eras of the same house
4water puzzles
  • All gameplay in Unreal C++: interaction and hold-to-push, the era director, the four water puzzles, the rising pool, family dialogue and the final family-portrait shot.
  • The pool surface shows the other era through a SceneCapture from the player's view; stepping in triggers a wipe transition that blends light, fog, colour grading, rain and sound between the two eras.
  • Python editor scripts build both versions of the house and the village in one pass, and generate textures, terrain, trees, props and sound.
  • Lumen GI and reflections, virtual shadow maps and Nanite; headless test, screenshot and frame-time benchmark modes.
  • Unreal Engine 5
  • C++
  • Python
  • Lumen
  • Nanite
